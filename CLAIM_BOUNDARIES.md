# Claim boundaries

## Established in the finite construction

1. The FORGE-L q=2 slice can be promoted to a finite oriented gauge 2-complex.
2. For the implemented k=4, q=2, face-cycle rule, the raw plaquette count satisfies P = 12 C at the audited scales.
3. Wilson-type finite measures are gauge invariant for compact gauge groups.
4. A fixed consecutive-generation rewrite history defines a finite Euclidean slab with temporal plaquettes.
5. The vertical transport graph is a forest and admits temporal gauge fixing.
6. The mirrored fixed-history slab is reflection positive by factorization.
7. The same-boundary round-trip operator T = R*R is positive and self-adjoint.
8. On the persistent core, the round-trip operator is norm-controlled relative to the reference heat-kernel operator.
9. Finite-volume SU(2) spectra and Monte Carlo observables are computationally reproducible within the stated tolerances.

## Conjectured, not proved

1. Existence of a cylindrically consistent multi-generation measure.
2. Reflection positivity for arbitrary asynchronous histories without mirrored-history pairing.
3. A nontrivial four-dimensional continuum limit.
4. Satisfaction of all Osterwalder–Schrader axioms in that limit.
5. Universality under rewrite order, seed, closure choice, and coarse-graining details.
6. A positive physical mass gap bounded away from zero uniformly as a_n -> 0 and volume -> infinity.

## Permissible public claim

“FORGE-YM supplies a constructive, falsifiable candidate framework for the Yang–Mills existence and mass-gap problem, together with finite reflection-positive transfer constructions and explicit continuum proof obligations.”

## Impermissible claim at v0.3

“FORGE-YM has proved or solved the Yang–Mills Millennium Problem.”
